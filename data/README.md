# Bengaluru place catalogue

`bengaluru-places.csv` is a research draft with 165 candidate places across eight discovery categories. `Bengaluru_Places_Catalog.xlsx` is the same catalogue in a review-friendly workbook.

The list is a broad starter set, not an exhaustive census. Place names, neighbourhoods and short descriptions were assembled from public Bengaluru/Karnataka tourism pages, venue pages and city directories. Every row remains a candidate: confirm that the venue is operating, identify the exact branch, and verify hours, access rules and current prices before showing it as a live recommendation.

Pricing is an indicative category-level band, not a verified menu or ticket price. Mood and ambience are editorial discovery cues, not measured attributes or guarantees about crowd levels. The `Qloo entity ID` column is intentionally blank until each venue is resolved against Qloo. Use the venue lookup link to check the exact place and branch.

Primary research references:

- Government of Karnataka, Bengaluru Urban tourism: <https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/tourism/>
- Karnataka Tourism, Bengaluru: <https://karnatakatourism.org/en/destinations/bengaluru/>
- Karnataka Tourism, Paper & Pie cafe: <https://karnatakatourism.org/en/experiences/paper-pie-cafe/>
- District, PLaY Arena: <https://www.district.in/events/play-arena-bengaluru-2025-buy-tickets>

Do not treat this draft as an OpenStreetMap extract. A wide Overpass query was attempted during research but could not be rendered reliably in the browser. If OpenStreetMap-derived records are added later, retain attribution and comply with ODbL.
